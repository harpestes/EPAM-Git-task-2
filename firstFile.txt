Classic Weekend Pancake Recipe
Mix 2 cups of all-purpose flour in a large glass bowl.
Add 2 tablespoons of brown sugar and a pinch of salt.
Crack one large or two eggs into the dry ingredients.
Pour in 1,5 cup of whole milk and stir gently until combined.
Melt 2 tablespoons of butter in a frying pan over medium heat.
Pour the batter onto the hot pan to form small circles.
Cook for 2 minutes until small bubbles appear on the surface.
Flip the pancakes and cook for another minute until golden brown.
Serve warm with maple syrup and fresh blueberries.
