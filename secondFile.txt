Morning Walk Journal
I woke up early today, right around 5:00 AM.
The weather was clear with a bright blue sky.
I put on my old red running shoes and headed out the door.
The neighborhood was quiet, except for a dog barking in the distance.
I walked down to the local park and sat on the wooden bench.
I watched a squirrel quickly climb up a tall oak tree.
After resting for a bit, I grabbed a hot coffee from the corner cafe.
The coffee was a bit bitter, so I added two packets of sugar.
I walked back home, feeling ready to start a productive Monday.
